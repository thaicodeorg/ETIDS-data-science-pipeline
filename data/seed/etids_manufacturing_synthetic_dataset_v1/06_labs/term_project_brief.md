# Term Project Brief: Integrated Manufacturing Data Science Pipeline

Students should choose one business problem from the dataset and deliver an end-to-end pipeline.

Recommended tracks:
1. Predictive maintenance from IIoT sensor data.
2. Quality-risk modeling with explainable AI and fairness review.
3. Delivery-risk prediction with dashboard evidence.
4. RAG assistant for manufacturing SOP, data quality rules, and maintenance notes.
5. Edge analytics and agentic AI workflow for production monitoring.
6. Blockchain traceability audit for material lots and quality gates.

Expected deliverables:
- Problem statement and research gap.
- Raw data profiling and data quality controls.
- Feature engineering and analytical base table.
- Model or dashboard output.
- Governance, ethics, and audit discussion.
- Short presentation and reproducible code.
